Session 4 - Family Therapy

Present: Isla Ferreira, Carla Ferreira (mother), Bruno Ferreira (father).
Isla's brother, Noah Ferreira, joined for the second half of the session.

Carla opened the session by describing a difficult week at home. She said
mornings before school had become a flashpoint, with Isla refusing to get
dressed and Carla feeling she had run out of patience. You can reach Carla
most afternoons on 01632 960 226 if a change of appointment is needed.

Bruno spoke about his own upbringing and said he wanted things to be
different for his children. He described feeling shut out of the morning
routine and agreed to take a more active role from next week.

Noah joined for the second half of the session. Noah is fourteen and
attends the same school as before. He was quiet for most of the discussion
but engaged well when asked directly about the summer holidays. He
confirmed he did not feel caught in the middle of his parents'
disagreements.

Isla drew a picture of the family during the session and was able to name
a feeling for each family member. She continues to present with
generalised anxiety and some school avoidance, though attendance has
improved this term.

Plan: continue fortnightly family sessions. Individual session for Isla to
be arranged separately.
