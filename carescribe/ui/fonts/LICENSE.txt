These files are vendored deliberately. The app must make no network request at
runtime, and the previous stylesheet pulled these faces from fonts.googleapis.com
on every launch -- before sign-in, and while the app's own docstring said it made
"no network calls of any kind". Fetching them at build time and shipping them is
what makes that claim true while keeping the type identity DESIGN.md pins.
